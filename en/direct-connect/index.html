Why Direct Connect

Hybrid workloads need a predictable network

An internet VPN is quick to set up, but its path and latency change from moment to moment. Large data migrations, real-time business systems and regulated traffic belong on a dedicated path with guaranteed quality.

Consistent

Consistent performance

Dedicated bandwidth, unaffected by public internet congestion, keeps latency and throughput predictable.

Private

Private path

Traffic stays on the AWS global network. Add encryption with MACsec and IPsec.

Cost

Lower transfer cost

Data transfer out over Direct Connect is priced lower than data transfer out to the internet.

Connection stack

One circuit, three layers

Direct Connect splits a physical circuit into virtual interfaces with VLANs, and each interface exchanges routes over BGP. The stack builds up from the physical layer at the bottom.

L3Routing

BGP peering

Each VIF runs an eBGP session to exchange routes. Control routing with AS_PATH and Local Preference communities, and detect failures quickly with BFD. View exchanged routes with their AS_PATH and communities in the console or with the ListVirtualInterfaceRoutes API.

Features

Key features

Global reach

Direct Connect Gateway

Reach VPCs and transit gateways in multiple Regions over a single connection. No separate circuit per Region.

Hub & spoke

Transit Gateway integration

Connect to Transit Gateway through a transit VIF and a DX gateway to route many VPCs from one point.

Global WAN

Cloud WAN integration

Attach a DX gateway directly to an AWS Cloud WAN core network and manage on-premises routes with segment policies.

Site to site

SiteLink

Connect Direct Connect locations directly over the AWS backbone. Branch-to-branch traffic flows without passing through a Region.

Encryption

MACsec · Private IP VPN

Use IEEE 802.1AE MACsec on dedicated connections at supported locations, or run a private IP Site-to-Site VPN over a transit VIF.

Public services

Public VIF

Reach public AWS endpoints such as Amazon S3 without crossing the internet.

Reference architecture

From on-premises to VPC

Your router meets AWS at a Direct Connect location. The DX gateway receives the VIFs and propagates routes to VPCs and transit gateways in multiple Regions, while the public VIF leads to public AWS services.

Resiliency toolkit

Match resiliency to business criticality

The Resiliency Toolkit in the Direct Connect console guides you through ordering connections that match a recommended model. The SLA applies when you follow the recommended configuration.

99.99%

Maximum Resiliency

Place circuits on separate devices at two or more Direct Connect locations. This model withstands device, circuit and location failures and is recommended for critical workloads.

99.9%

High Resiliency

Place one circuit at each of two Direct Connect locations. Connectivity survives the failure of an entire location.

Getting started

How to set up a dedicated connection

  1. STEP 1

    Request a connection

    In the console, choose a location and port speed and request the connection.

  2. STEP 2

    Get the LOA-CFA

    Once AWS allocates the port, download the LOA-CFA from the console.

  3. STEP 3

    Cross-connect

    Give the LOA-CFA to your colocation provider or circuit partner to complete the physical connection.

  4. STEP 4

    Create a VIF

    Choose the VLAN, BGP ASN and peer IPs, then create the VIF.

  5. STEP 5

    Verify BGP

    Configure BGP on your router, then check the session state and the VIF's received and advertised routes.

Pricing · Standard model

Pay-as-you-go: pay for what you use

This is the default pricing model for all connections. You pay for port hours and for data transferred out of AWS, with no setup fee or minimum charge. Circuit partner and colocation costs are billed separately. For 10 · 100 Gbps dedicated connections you can choose the flat-rate model on the next page instead.

Pricing · New model (September 2026)

Flat-rate: a fixed hourly rate that includes DTO

A separate pricing model available only for 10 · 100 Gbps dedicated connections. You pay only an hourly rate set by bandwidth and tier, and data transferred out (DTO) from the Regions your tier covers has no per-GB charge. The monthly charge is the hourly rate for your bandwidth and tier × hours the connection is provisioned, rounded up to the hour. Available at Direct Connect locations in all commercial Regions except the China Regions.

Operational considerations for flat-rate
ConsiderationDetails
port-pairPairs two dedicated connections on different devices or locations with the same bandwidth and tier. One side is standby, so a 10 Gbps port-pair gives 10 Gbps of usable bandwidth, not 20. You can add existing connections to a Resiliency Group to form a port-pair, or keep them as individual flat-rate connections.
Multi-Region connectivityAttaching a DX gateway, Transit Gateway or Cloud WAN can bring in traffic from multiple Regions. Before attaching, confirm your tier covers every Region you need; expanding to a new Region may require a higher tier.
Switching modelsSwitch any time with Change Billing Mode in the console or the UpdateConnectionsBillingMode API, without interrupting traffic. Within an account the change applies per location: switching one connection switches every connection at that location.
